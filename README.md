# Norsk social study quiz

Free practice quiz for the Norwegian samfunnskunnskapsprøve, in English and Bokmål.

570 multiple-choice questions on the 57 lessons of samfunnskunnskap.no.
Runs in the browser; progress is saved only in your browser.

Questions are AI-generated and may contain mistakes. Not affiliated with
samfunnskunnskap.no or HK-dir. Free and provided as is, with no guarantees.
